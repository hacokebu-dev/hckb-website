If you've launched multiple apps, you might want a single page that showcases your entire collection rather than sharing individual links one by one. I found myself wondering how to access that "developer-only" list page, and it turns out there’s a simple way to do it.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;  
## 1. Access the App Store Marketing Tools
<img src="/assets/asmyapp/asmyapp1.png">

First, visit Apple's official marketing toolkit:
<a href="https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/en-us/app-store/us" target="_blank">toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/en-us/app-store/us</a>

Once there, **search for any one of your apps** to enter its specific asset page.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;  

## 2. Click Your Developer Name
<img src="/assets/asmyapp/asmyapp2.jpg">

Right below the app's title, you'll see **your developer name in blue**. Click it. This link acts as the gateway to your personal storefront on the App Store.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;  

## 3. Find Your Dedicated Portfolio Link
<img src="/assets/asmyapp/asmyapp3.jpg">

The page you are now on displays every app you've ever released. 

At the bottom, under **"Content Link,"** you'll find the unique URL. This is the direct address to your personal app list—perfect for sharing with users or adding to your website bio.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;  

**Summary**
- Use the <a href="https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/en-us/app-store/us" target="_blank">App Store Marketing Tools</a> site to search for your app.
- Click your developer name to jump to your full "App List" page.
- Copy the "Content Link" at the bottom to get your dedicated portfolio address.
