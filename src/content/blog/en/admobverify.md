To receive payouts from Google AdMob, you must go through a mandatory verification process. First, you need to complete identity verification. Once your earnings reach a specific threshold, you will then be required to verify your address.
&nbsp;

The address verification process is surprisingly old-school. Google sends a physical mailer to the address listed in your account, and you must enter the PIN provided in that letter into AdMob to complete the verification. It felt a bit surreal that a global tech giant like Google still relies on snail mail, almost as if they want to make sure you really earn that payout.
&nbsp;
<img src="/assets/admb/admb01.jpg">
In my case, the PIN mailer was sent out on June 10th and arrived on June 25th—taking exactly two weeks. Looking at the postmark, it appeared to have been shipped from Malaysia.
&nbsp;

I was actually a bit anxious before it arrived. AdMob only allows you to request a replacement PIN up to three times. I kept worrying about it getting lost in transit or misplaced, so it was a huge relief when it finally showed up in my mailbox.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;

<img src="/assets/admb/admb02.jpg">
Once you have the letter, the actual verification step is straightforward. Following the instructions inside, log into AdMob, navigate to 'Payments → Verification check', and enter the 6-digit PIN. The address status updates to verified instantly.
&nbsp;

&nbsp;

<img src="/assets/admb/admb03.jpg">
<img src="/assets/admb/admb04.jpg">
The notice warns that if you don't verify your PIN within 4 months, ad serving will be suspended. I'm not entirely sure if this 4-month window starts from the date the mailer was sent or not, but it's definitely something you don't want to procrastinate on.
&nbsp;

With that, my AdMob address verification mission is officially complete. Now, I'm looking forward to finally seeing that first payout hit my account!
